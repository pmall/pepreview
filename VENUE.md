# Target Venue: PLOS Computational Biology (Review)

Checked 2026-11-19 against PLOS Computational Biology public author guidelines.

## Journal fit and scope

- Publishes computational approaches (including AI and machine learning) applied
  to living systems at all scales; de novo binder design fits directly within
  scope (`journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/s/journal-information`).
- Open access, peer-reviewed, no length-based page charges.
- Reviews are peer-reviewed front-section articles; confirm with the editorial
  office (`ploscompbiol@plos.org`) that an unsolicited Review on this topic is
  welcome before submission.

## Article type guidelines (Review)

From PLOS Computational Biology article-type guidance
(`.../s/other-article-types`):

- **Topic:** Covers a rapidly advancing, topical area of broad interest to the
  biology community not extensively covered elsewhere.
- **Length and display items:** Aim for **3,000–6,000 words** of main text and
  **2–3 figures or tables** (no hard submission cap, but concise presentation is
  expected).
- **Expectation:** Go beyond a list of paper summaries by organizing concepts
  clearly, comparing methodological approaches, and synthesizing open challenges
  supported by literature across multiple groups.
- **Structure:** Title, Authors, Affiliations, Abstract, Introduction, Main Text
  (maximum **3 heading levels**), Conclusions, Acknowledgments, References.
- **Placement of figures and tables:** Insert captions and tables immediately
  after the first paragraph that cites them.

## Submission and formatting rules

From PLOS submission guidelines (`.../s/submission-guidelines`):

- **File format:** Initial submission as a single PDF containing text and
  figures (DOC/DOCX/RTF also accepted); double-spaced, single column, English.
- **Title page:** Title, authors, affiliations, followed by the Abstract.
- **References:** Numbered in order of appearance, cited in square brackets
  (e.g., `[19]`, Vancouver style, not author–year). Internal `[@bibkey]` keys in
  `literature/map.md` map directly to `literature/refs.bib` for compilation.
- **Required statements:** Data Availability, Financial Disclosure, Competing
  Interests, Author Contributions, ORCID for the corresponding author.
- **Preprints:** Encouraged; PLOS can auto-post a bioRxiv preprint during
  submission.
- **AI-use disclosure (PLOS policy):** AI tools may assist in drafting or
  analysis but cannot be listed as authors. Any AI contribution must be
  disclosed in Methods or Acknowledgments (tool name, role, and how outputs were
  verified against primary sources).
